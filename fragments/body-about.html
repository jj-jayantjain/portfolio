Professional introduction

Hi, I'm Jayant.

I've spent the last 10+ years building on Adobe Experience Manager — first as a developer shipping components and templates, now as a technical lead architecting migrations and automation for enterprise teams. Along the way I picked up 3+ years of hands-on JavaScript, HTML and CSS depth, a working knowledge of Java microservices, and — more recently — a genuine enthusiasm for what Generative AI can do inside enterprise workflow tools.

What hasn't changed is how I work: understand the business problem first, design a solution that a team can actually maintain, and stay close enough to the code to know when a shortcut will cost more later. I'm equally comfortable in a CRXDE console, a stakeholder review, or an OpenAI prompt template.

Career journey

From components to architecture to automation.

2016

Learning the platform, one component at a time

Started as an AEM Developer on a migration and code-optimization engagement for Apple Incorporation — moving from AEM 6.1 to 6.3 and modernizing components from SCR to OSGi annotations.

2018–22

Owning bigger surface area

Took on DAM performance work for Lululemon, then stepped into an AEM Lead role for TLC Digitech's premium hotel membership platform — my first end-to-end solution ownership.

2022–24

Enterprise commerce at scale

Senior AEM Developer on Abbott's ANAPOLLO program, integrating Magento Commerce via GraphQL into custom AEM components for the Abbott Store and Glucerna Store.

2025–Present

Leading the shift to Edge Delivery and GenAI

Team Lead on American University's CommonSpot-to-Adobe-EDS migration, and builder of a GenAI automation layer connecting OpenAI to Adobe Workfront Fusion.
